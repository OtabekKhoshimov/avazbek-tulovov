Where did today’s Registon begin?

«To start something big, you first need a big goal.»

2013. Tashkent.

I was tutoring university applicants in a rented house. The students kept coming, and we ran out of space.
We could not afford new desks. My student Alisher and I went to the Yangiobod market.
We bought two old desks.

Back then we had no big building, no modern equipment, no investor.
But we did have one thing.
The belief that education can change people’s lives.

Looking back now, I understand that even then Registon’s greatest asset was not buildings or money.
Our greatest asset was our goal, and our readiness to work for it.

Many people think you need a lot of money to start something big.
I think differently.
To start something big, you first need a big goal. The money and the opportunities are found along the way, as you keep searching.

To young people who are waiting for every condition to be perfect before they start, I have one thing to say:
What matters is not what you have in your hands, but what you do with it.

What small step towards your big dream did you take today?
