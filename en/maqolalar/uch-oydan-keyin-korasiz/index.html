“You’ll see in three months!”

«A leader’s confidence is tested not on good days, but in the hardest situations.»

17 May 2023.

I am standing on the sixth floor of the first Registon school in Tashkent.
Around me are concrete columns and building materials. It is scorching hot. Very little about the building looks like a school yet.
But we are planning to admit students in September.

Parents come and look at the construction.
“Can this building really become a school in three months?”
“Will you make it before the school year starts?”
And I give the same answer every time:
“Yes, of course. You’ll see in three months!”

To be honest, I had my own worries at the time.
After all, this was not just about a building, but about the future of hundreds of children and the trust of their parents.

In those days I came to understand something deeply.
A leader’s confidence is tested not on good days, but in the hardest situations.
Confidence does not mean ignoring problems.
On the contrary, it means seeing the problems more clearly than anyone else and taking responsibility for solving them.

I have a question for leaders who are starting something big today:
How much do you yourself believe in the future you are asking your team to believe in?
