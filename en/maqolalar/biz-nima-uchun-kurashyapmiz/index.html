What are we fighting for?

«We want to change the world through education.»

Sometimes I ask myself a question.
Why do we work so hard?
New branches, schools, teacher training, curriculum development...
Every day a new problem, a new responsibility.
After all, couldn’t we run a business and make money in some other field?

But for me, Registon has never been simply a business that makes money.
I believe that education changes people’s lives.
I have seen an ordinary village boy reach great opportunities through knowledge.
I have witnessed young people’s lives change completely just because they met a good teacher.

That is why I cannot treat education merely as an economic activity.
Education is one of the most important fields that decides the future of a nation.
The generation we raise today is the society we will have tomorrow.
If we can raise young people who are educated, think independently, love their country and take responsibility, Uzbekistan’s future will match that.

In Registon schools and learning centres, one goal of ours is written on the wall:
“To make Uzbekistan’s education the best in the world!”
I know very well that this goal is not easy to reach.
Perhaps it will take our whole lives.

But I believe in one thing.
A person should devote their life to work that is useful not only to themselves, but to the future of others.
We want to change the world through education.
There is still a great deal to do on this path.

What do you think we need to change first to make Uzbekistan’s education the best in the world?
