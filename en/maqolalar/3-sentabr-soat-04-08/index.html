3 September 2023. 04:08

«A school can be built. Turning it into a true place of learning takes work every single day.»

I got into my car and looked at the clock.
04:08.
In a few hours the first private Registon school in Tashkent was due to open.
And we had been cleaning the building until four in the morning.
Construction dust, placing the furniture, the last preparations...
A building that four or five months earlier had been nothing but concrete columns had become a real school.

I went home, washed, put on my formal clothes.
I looked in the mirror.
Could you see the tiredness on my face?
Inside, a mix of feelings.
Joy. Excitement. Worry. Gratitude.

Because this was not just the opening of a new building.
It was the result of three years of preparation, the work of hundreds of people and the promise we had made to parents.

At around 7:30 in the morning I returned to the school.
Students, parents, teachers...
In that moment I felt something very deeply.
The best moment of reaching a big goal is not seeing the result, but realising that the work you put in along the way was not wasted.

But our work did not end with the opening of the school.
On the contrary, the greatest responsibility was only beginning.
Because a school can be built. Turning it into a true place of learning takes work every single day.
