Without my team I am nobody!

«A strong leader’s greatest achievement is to unite people who are even stronger than themselves around one goal.»

2023. Our first school is still under construction.

Parents doubted the school would open on time.
And we were fighting to turn a building that was still a concrete frame into a school.
In those days the phrase “concrete frame” took on a different meaning for me.

Just as a building’s strength depends on its columns, an organisation’s strength depends on its team.
Registon’s real pillars were never its buildings. They were its people.
People who overcame hardships with us, who stood by us in good days and bad, and who believed in our big goal.

When I think about it, it would be unfair to call Registon’s results today the personal success of any one person.
They are the work of hundreds, thousands of people.
A leader can show an organisation the way. But unless they walk that way together with the team, the big destination is never reached.

Some leaders attribute all the success to themselves.
I believe in one thing:
A strong leader’s greatest achievement is to unite people who are even stronger than themselves around one goal.

Registon’s success today and tomorrow depends on those people.
