Finding the first student was not easy either

«Behind every customer stands a person who trusted you.»

2013.

We are trying to expand our tutoring work in Tashkent.
We have no way of reaching thousands of people through social media the way we do today.
We write a flyer on plain paper.
“Preparation centre for university applicants. Native language and literature. History.”
We paste these flyers along the streets.

Among our first students were two girls who had come from Kattakurgan to Tashkent to prepare for admission.
Their trust meant a great deal to us.
Because at the time we had no well-known brand, no big building, no long list of results to show.
We relied only on our knowledge and our hard work.

Today I realise how big a role the first customers play in an organisation’s growth.
They do not just pay you money.
They trust you at a time when nobody else does yet.
And living up to that trust is a great responsibility.

I believe every entrepreneur should remember their first customers.
Because as a business grows, there is a risk of forgetting the most important thing.
Behind every customer stands a person who trusted you.

Those very first students have their share in where Registon stands today.
