What is a parent’s trust worth?

«Parents’ trust is earned not by advertising, but by results and responsibility.»

August 2023.

About a month remains before the first Registon school opens.
We are planning to admit 220 students.
But we have not yet enrolled even 100.
On top of that, construction work is still going on in the school building.

Parents come to learn about our school. They ask about our programmes, our teachers, our facilities.
But in most of their eyes there is one question:
“Can I trust you with my child?”

At the time I understood parents’ doubts very well.
They were about to entrust us not with an object, but with the most precious person in their lives.

Today, in running the school, I still pay special attention to this.
Parents’ trust is earned not by advertising, but by results and responsibility.
A beautiful building, modern equipment and good advertising can make parents interested in a school.
But if they do not see positive change in their child’s knowledge, character and outlook, that trust will not last long.

That is why, for me, one of a school’s most important indicators is the trust parents place in us.
Admitting a student is the beginning of success. Living up to their trust is the real responsibility.
