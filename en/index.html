About me

From a village boy to the founder of Registon

I grew up in an ordinary village in the Pakhtachi district of Samarkand region, in a farmer’s family. The bookshelf in our home and the works of the Jadid thinkers shaped the way I see the world.

I studied philosophy at the National University of Uzbekistan and went on to a master’s degree. From 2011 I tutored university applicants in Tashkent. In 2013, lessons for a handful of students in a rented house, taught together with my own student, grew into the Registon education project.

Today the Registon education project is a single path from kindergarten to a profession: private schools in Tashkent, Samarkand, Fergana and Bukhara, learning centres in 14 regions, a lyceum, a publishing house, online learning, an exam centre, a medical college, and the Turkiston school launched in 2026. And I still think of myself, first of all, as a teacher.

What I believe in

  • ResultsThe true value of education lies not in the number of lessons taught, but in the change that happens in the student.
  • TeamA strong leader’s greatest achievement is to unite people who are even stronger than themselves around one goal.
  • TrustParents’ trust is earned not by advertising, but by results and responsibility.
  • KnowledgeI want the world to come to this land for knowledge again, as it once did.

Journey

From two old desks to schools

  1. 2013
    The beginning

    Tashkent, a rented house. Two old desks from the Yangiobod market. Hand-posted street flyers and the first students.

  2. 2014
    Registon learning centre

    The first official project. It started with 600 students and some 20 staff.

  3. 2015
    1,500 students

    The Chilonzor branch passed 1,500 students — at the time only a handful of centres in Tashkent had more than a thousand.

  4. 2019
    Regbooks

    Our own publishing house for university applicants opened.

  5. 2020
    The test

    Lockdown. The classrooms emptied, the obligations remained. The year that taught us an organisation’s real strength shows in a crisis.

  6. 2023
    The first school

    3 September. A building that had been a concrete frame three months earlier opened as the first private Registon school in Tashkent. The online and exam centres launched the same year.

  7. 2024
    From kindergarten to college

    The Registon kindergarten and medical college opened. The schools reached Samarkand, Fergana and Bukhara.

  8. 2026
    Turkiston and 30,000+

    Built on twelve years of Registon’s experience, the Turkiston school was launched. Student numbers passed 30,000.
