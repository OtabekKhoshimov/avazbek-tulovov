# Is getting into university the biggest goal in life?
> Getting into university should not be the goal, but one stage of preparing for a bigger life.

2011–2013.

I was tutoring university applicants in Tashkent.
I talked a lot with my students. I tried to learn about their dreams, their plans, their thoughts about the future.
And I noticed one thing.
**For most of them, getting into university had become the biggest goal in life.**
“If only I get in!”

And then what?
What will you do after university? Which field will you become a specialist in? How will you be useful to society?
Most of them had no clear answer to these questions.

I thought the same then as I do now.
**Getting into university should not be the goal, but one stage of preparing for a bigger life.**
Sometimes we prepare our children for the exam so intensely that we forget to prepare them for life.
A high score matters. Getting into a prestigious university is a real achievement.
But it is still no guarantee of a successful life.

I want the graduates of our schools not merely to get into good universities, but to find their place in life.
**The final result of education is not a diploma, but a person’s ability to benefit themselves and society through what they know.**

**How often do you talk with your child about their life after they get into university?**
