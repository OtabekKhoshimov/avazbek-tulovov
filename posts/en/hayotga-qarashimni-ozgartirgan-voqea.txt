# The event that changed how I see life
> An educated person can change their own life. An educated generation can change the future of a whole society.

1999. I am in the seventh grade.

We walked four kilometres from home to school.
There were four of us friends. We went to school together and came back together.
One day our friend Aziz stopped coming to school.
At first we thought he had caught a cold.
Then we learned he had a serious heart condition.
His family was struggling. The treatment he needed was hard to get.

One day my father and I went to see how he was.
There was a crowd at the gate.
Aziz had died.

That day shook me deeply.
**Why should a child be deprived of life’s opportunities because of where they were born or how much their family has?**
Why should village children have fewer opportunities than children in the city?
These questions troubled me for a long time.

Later I understood that one of the most important ways to reduce many of society’s problems is quality education.
**An educated person can change their own life. An educated generation can change the future of a whole society.**
When I chose education as my field, these thoughts strongly influenced my decision.

I still remember those village children today.
And I ask myself:
**How many children’s lives will our work today change for the better tomorrow?**
