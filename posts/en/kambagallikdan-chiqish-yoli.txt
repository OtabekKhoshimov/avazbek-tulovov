# What is the surest way out of poverty?
> Investing in a child’s education is investing in the whole family’s opportunities tomorrow.

I was studying for my master’s degree.

Our professor took us to a neighbourhood not far from Tashkent.
Quite close to the capital.
But when I saw how people lived there, I was stunned.
No gas. Drinking water had to be brought in. The men of many families had gone abroad to work.

A question came to me then.
**Why are the opportunities of people living so close to the capital so limited?**
Searching for an answer, I thought again and at length about the role of education in society.

Imagine one family.
The father works abroad and sends money to his children.
If that money is spent only on daily needs, the family’s future may not change much.
But what if the children get a good education and grow into strong professionals?
They can change not only their own lives, but the opportunities of the next generation.

**One of the most sustainable ways to break the chain of poverty is quality education.**
Of course, jobs, infrastructure and economic opportunity are needed too.
But without knowledge, it is hard to make use even of the opportunities that exist.

That is why I do not regard money spent on education as an ordinary expense.
**Investing in a child’s education is investing in the whole family’s opportunities tomorrow.**

**Do you think families give their children’s education enough priority?**
