# What if everything stops one day?
> An organisation’s real strength shows not when everything is going well, but when it faces an unexpected crisis.

March 2020.

A lockdown was declared because of the coronavirus.
Schools, learning centres and many organisations were forced to suspend their work.
Classrooms that had been full of students the day before stood empty.
Some of the teachers left for the regions.
Parents could not send their children to lessons.

And in front of us stood an enormous question:
**What do we do now?**
When an organisation stops working, its obligations do not stop.
There are employees. There is rent. There is responsibility to parents.
In such a situation, the usual methods of management are no longer enough.
You have to look for new solutions.

That period taught me an important lesson.
**An organisation’s real strength shows not when everything is going well, but when it faces an unexpected crisis.**
A leader’s job is not only to manage growth.
In hard times they must be able to hold the team together, find solutions and keep faith in the future.

Neither in business nor in life can everything be planned in advance.
But how we respond to difficulties is in our own hands.
**A crisis can weaken an organisation, or it can make it stronger.** Much of that is decided by the choices of the leader and the team.
