# Where did our marketing begin?
> Marketing can bring a person through your door. But what keeps them with you is results.

2012–2013.

No advertising systems or big marketing teams like today.
There was one simple way to attract students.
We printed flyers on paper.
In the evening we prepared 250–300 of them.
At 5:30 in the morning we got up and pasted them on lamp posts along the streets of Chilonzor and Uchtepa.
By midday most of them had been torn down.
The next day, the same all over again.

My student Alisher and I repeated this many times.
Back then, even a single phone call was a big opportunity for us.
**Because behind every call was a young person who wanted to learn.**

Today marketing offers countless possibilities.
Social networks, advertising platforms, automated systems...
But in my view the core principle of marketing has not changed.
**You must know exactly how your product or service improves people’s lives.**

Back then our goal was not simply to recruit students, but to open the door to higher education for young people.
I still think the same way today.
**Marketing can bring a person through your door. But what keeps them with you is results.**
