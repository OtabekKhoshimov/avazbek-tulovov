# What did university give me?
> Memorising knowledge and being an educated person are not the same thing.

I often think back to my student years.
I studied at the Faculty of Philosophy of the National University of Uzbekistan.
After classes I used to go to the National Library and read a great deal.
In my first week at university I wrote a list of the books I wanted to read.
Over time I read every one of them.

But university gave me more than the knowledge in books.
The worldviews of the professors who taught us differed sharply from one another.
Sometimes we heard completely opposite opinions on the same question.
**That pushed me to think, to ask questions, to analyse every idea.**

Today, when I think about education, it is this experience I remember.
**A good education should not only give students ready answers. It should also teach them to ask the right questions.**

In our schools we give children a great deal of information.
But can they analyse that information independently?
Can they justify their own opinion?
Can they listen to a different view and draw conclusions from it?

I believe this is one of the most important tasks facing the modern school.
**Memorising knowledge and being an educated person are not the same thing.**
