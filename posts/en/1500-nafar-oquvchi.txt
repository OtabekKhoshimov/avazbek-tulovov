# 1,500 students. How did we get there?
> Real success is not growth itself, but keeping quality as you grow.

2015.

The number of students at Registon’s Chilonzor branch passed 1,500.
At the time, there were only a handful of learning centres in Tashkent with more than a thousand students.
For us it was a huge result.
After all, just two years earlier we had been teaching a few students in a rented house.
The days of buying old desks and pasting flyers on the streets were still fresh in our memory.
And now thousands of young people were putting their trust in us.

How did we achieve this?
With a big advertising budget?
Thanks to a modern building or expensive equipment?
All of these may matter to some extent.
But I see the main reason elsewhere.

**We had a team that believed in one goal and was ready to work for it.**
The teachers worked for results.
The leaders did not run from problems.
We tried to live up to the trust of every new student.

In those years I understood one thing.
**A big result comes not from one person’s talent, but from many people moving in the same direction.**

Even today, when I assess our branches, I try not to look only at the number of students.
Because if there is no quality behind the numbers, that kind of growth will not last.
**Real success is not growth itself, but keeping quality as you grow.**
