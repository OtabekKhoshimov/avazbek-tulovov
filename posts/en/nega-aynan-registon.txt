# Why “Registon”?
> We wanted to build not just a learning centre, but a project that brings knowledge back to the centre of society.

Once upon a time we were looking for a name for our education project.
Turkiston, Turon, Ulugbek, Mir Arab, Dorul ilm...
We discussed many options.
In the end we chose “Registon”.

This was not simply a search for a beautiful or catchy name.
**For me, Registan is, first of all, a symbol of knowledge and enlightenment.**
Centuries ago, great madrasas stood around Registan Square in Samarkand. People came from many lands in search of knowledge.
In that age, places of learning were among the most important centres of society.

I dream of such a society.
I want our schools to become the most respected places in the city.
A society where parents care less about which wedding hall their child will celebrate in, and more about which school their child attends and what they learn there.
**Because a nation’s future is decided by what it pays the most attention to today.**

When we chose the name Registon, there was no guarantee that any of these dreams would come true.
But our direction was clear.
**We wanted to build not just a learning centre, but a project that brings knowledge back to the centre of society.**

We are still working towards that goal today.
