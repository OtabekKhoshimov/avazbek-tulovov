# What do we navigate by?
> I want our attention to knowledge and our respect for education to stand above any luxury.

May 2023.

The Registon school was still under construction. Looking out of the window, I could see one of the city’s most famous and lavish wedding halls, Versal, right across the street. Whenever someone asked for our address, we answered, as everyone did: “Next to the Versal wedding hall.”

One day I stood on the construction site and looked around for a long time. Building materials everywhere, workers, unfinished walls... But in my mind I saw a completely different picture: a school full of children, young people learning, parents hopeful about their children’s future. And at that moment a thought crossed my mind: **“One day people will find their way here not by the wedding hall, but by the Registon school.”** I sincerely believed it.

Because there have been such times in our history. Five hundred years ago, one of Samarkand’s most important landmarks was Registan Square. Registan was not just a square. Around it stood madrasas, the great centres of learning of their age. People came from distant lands in search of knowledge.

I keep wondering... When did we start navigating by wedding halls instead of centres of learning? Why can’t the most famous building in a city today be a school? After all, a society builds its landmarks around what it values. I have nothing against wedding halls. **But I want our attention to knowledge and our respect for education to stand above any luxury.**

On the walls of Registon schools we have written one goal: **“To make Uzbekistan’s education the best in the world!”** To some this may sound like big words.
But for me it is not just a slogan. It is my life, my dream, my conviction and my daily struggle.

We are not only building school buildings. We want to help build a society where knowledge is valued, teachers are honoured and parents take pride in their children’s learning. My dream is for schools in Uzbekistan to become the most important landmarks again. Not merely landmarks that show an address, but ones that show a whole generation the way to its future.

**What do you think: what does our society navigate by today?**
